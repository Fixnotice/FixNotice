Questions

Where does the information come from?

The City of Buffalo's open-data portal: the code-violations feed and the rental registry, both public records updated by the Department of Permit and Inspection Services. We read them; we don't change them. Always confirm with the department before acting on a notice.

Doesn't the city already notify me?

By mail, to the address on the tax roll, sometimes weeks later. A 2024 Buffalo News investigation found nearly half of registered rentals delinquent on fees, many because the notices went to old addresses. We email you the morning the record appears in the city's data.

I manage properties for other owners. Does this work?

Yes. Watch any address you're responsible for; the owner's name isn't required. The 60-property plan is built for managers, and larger portfolios are available by email.

How do I add or remove a property?

Reply to any FixNotice Watch email with the address. "Add 45 Elm St" or "remove 130 Alabama St" is enough. Changes are confirmed by email.

How do I cancel?

In your PayPal account under Subscriptions, any time, or reply STOP to any email.
