How it works

The city does the inspecting. We do the sorting. You do the estimates.

1

An inspector cites a property

Buffalo publishes every code violation and building permit as public data, usually within a day or two of the visit.

2

We match it to your trade

Each violation is read and sorted to the trade that fixes it, then paired with the owner's name and mailing address from state property records.

3

You get a ready-to-work list

A spreadsheet by 7 AM: address, what the inspector wrote, rental or not, owner contact. Highest-value leads sorted to the top. Never the same lead twice.

Questions

Where does the data come from?

The City of Buffalo's open-data portal (code violations, permits, rental registry) and New York State's property records (owner names and mailing addresses). All public. We do the daily sorting and matching so you don't have to.

Can I contact the owners?

Yes, within the normal rules. Mailing a letter is the simplest. Calls and texts have to follow the Do Not Call registry and the TCPA. Leads are provided as-is from public records.

How fresh are the leads?

Usually within 1 to 3 days of the inspection, and you never get the same lead twice.

What if there are no leads for my trade one day?

You'll get a short note instead of an empty list. Over a month, most trades see 50 to 130 leads.

How do I cancel?

In your PayPal account under Subscriptions, any time. Or email us and we'll do it the same day.

Other cities?

Buffalo first. Rochester, Syracuse and New York City are next; email us if you want one sooner.

Be the first contractor the owner hears from.

Most owners who get a violation notice call whoever they can find. Be the one who already knows.
